Daggerheart Card Sheet Extractor

This works 100% locally, the PDF is not uploaded to any server. Do not distribute your official PDFs.

This tool will automatically extract the images from your owned PDF for use in the Daggerheart [Unofficial] tabletop simulator addon

Pages

The range of pages in your PDF that contain the cards. For Core, you should get 4 sheets, and the last sheets should start with Troubador and end with a row of Halfling, Katari and Simiah. For Hope and Fear, you should get a single sheet that starts with Blighting Strike and ends with Werewolf. If this isn't the case, choose a preset below, or manually change the From and To page range to match your PDF.
